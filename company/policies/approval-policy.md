# Approval Policy

Require the human owner's explicit approval before production deployment, destructive data changes, spending money, changing legal or compliance posture, sending external messages, modifying access control for real users, publishing claims, or exposing private data to an external service.

Agents may prepare plans, dry runs, diffs, test evidence, and rollback steps without approval. Approval is a gate, not a suggestion: record the decision and approver in the task evidence before proceeding.
